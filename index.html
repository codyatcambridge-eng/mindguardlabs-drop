Objections, answered

I already have Jobber / ServiceTitan — why you?

Those run the job. We plug the leak before the job exists — missed-call SMS, follow-up that stops on reply, and social that keeps proof alive after the estimate. They stack; they don’t replace each other.

Who owns the Make.com scenario?

You do. We build in your account. You keep the logins — you type passwords. We leave you with a working scenario you can trigger without us.

How long until it works?

Install Intensive: half-day. Social Engine and Team Agent Stack: typically 1–2 weeks to live. You trigger Intensive once without us before we leave.

Do I need to understand AI?

No. You need to answer texts and approve posts. Grok and Hermes are assistants with memory and handoffs — not something you prompt from scratch every day.

Is Social Engine in the $497?

No. Intensive covers missed-call recovery and follow-up. Social Engine ($1,997) is a separate custom board — approve → multi-platform. Ask if you want both.

Remote vs Newnan / Coweta on-site?

Both. Coweta-area shops can get on-site Intensive. Remote works for the same installs over screen share. You still type your own passwords either way.
